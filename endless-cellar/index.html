    Endless Cellar¶

    Last updated: October 16, 2026

    A team-based instance where a party fights through 77 floors of monsters, mini-bosses and MVPs, with a Bio Laboratory MVP gauntlet and Pori Pori on the last floor.

    Quick Facts

    • Location: Captain Janssen in Prontera (/navi prontera 217/187), then Misty Island
    • Party Size: 6 person minimum
    • Base Level: 85
    • Time Limit: 4 hours
    • Run Limit: 3 per week (Instance Guide)

    Rules¶

    Rule Detail
    Death Normal: players can be resurrected and come back into the instance.
    Re-entry Original members of the party may leave the dungeon and re-enter as long as a member is still active within the dungeon. If all members exit, the instance will be deleted and the weekly run is consumed.
    Storage Not available inside the instance.
    RODEX Available inside the instance.
    Loot Drops as normal, with some modifications.
    Run Unlock Speak to Arc Pori Pori after the final boss. Leaving any other way doesn't unlock your next run.
    Monster Count Monsters remaining per floor are announced starting with 5 remaining.

    Getting There¶

    To get to the instance starting point, talk to Captain Janssen in Prontera (/navi prontera 217/187). Speak with him multiple times to initiate the journey.

    Once you unlock the Warp Helper on the island, Warpra takes you to Misty Island for 5,000z.

    A Kafra NPC on the island provides:

    • Personal Storage
    • Guild Storage
    • Return to Prontera

    On Misty Island, locate the Cellar Protection Stone NPC and select Enter Endless Cellar. All party members must be present on Misty Island when the Party Leader clicks the stone to start the dungeon.

    After the instance is initiated, every party member must type !ready in public chat within 60 seconds. If anyone doesn't, the whole party returns to their save point and the instance must be restarted. Read the Instance Guide for run limits and completion.

    Rewards¶

    After defeating Pori Pori, Arc Pori Pori appears and gives each party member a completion reward:

    Name Amount
    Old Card Album 1
    Bloody Branch 1
    Jewelry Box 1
    Poring Box 1
    Cookie Bag 1

    Claim Your Reward

    Talk to Arc Pori Pori before returning to Prontera. Exiting through him is required to unlock your next weekly run.
