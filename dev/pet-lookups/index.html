    Pet Lookups¶

    Last updated: October 11, 2026

    Open questions and confirmed values for the data in data/pets.yml. Not in the nav; it's for editors of the pet page.

    Egg images¶

    • Done: 104 egg icons downloaded from Divine Pride (static.divine-pride.net/images/items/item/<id>.png) into docs/img/Pet_System/<egg id>.png and data/pets.yml, and into game-data-tools/images/items/. Only the pets with no egg ID (Valkyrie Randgris) have no egg icon. Wrong egg IDs below would mean wrong icons.

    Egg IDs to confirm in game¶

    • Wild Rose 9141, Eddga 9142, Fire Golem 9131, Kiel D-01 9126, Chaotic Bapho Jr 9130: confirmed.
    • Wild Rose and Eddga egg icons downloaded.
    • Valkyrie Randgris egg is 9185. The name is "Randgris Egg", from monster-changes.md.
    • White Knight (evolved) may not be a real mob, so it has no mob ID. Egg 9134 and Mutating White Knight's 9133 match the client file.
    • Baphomet egg 9137: confirmed.

    Mob and item IDs¶

    • Evil Nymph: egg 9047 and bonus match your screenshot. Mob ID is 1416 (Wicked Nymph); the drop source uses it too (1564 is the no-EXP copy)
    • 12 custom pets had their mob ID taken from the sprite filename on the old page, not a database: Green Petite, Knife Goblin, Flail Goblin, Hammer Goblin, Wandering Duck Doll, Mutating White Knight, Yao Jun, Chaotic Bapho Jr, Blue Moon Loli Ruri, Contaminated Wanderer, Abysmal Knight, Fire Golem.
    • Poring's taming item is Unripe Apple (619); icon fixed.
    • Hornet's taming item is Beehive (102723, the client says Hornets like it), dropped by Hornet at 1%.
    • Names: Imp, Civil Servant, Chung E.

    • Tai-Zi (egg 9184): rotating event shop item. The price stays unknown; no mob ID, Pet Food, no bonus.
    • Event Token is item 7866, sold by Marley in Main Office. Blue Unicorn is 3 tokens.
    • Mochi icon added.
    • Rice Cake: mob 1815 and diet Green Herb come from Hercules and the client file. Confirm.

    Values taken from the emulators (not checked against uaRO)¶

    • Loyal intimacy is 900 (confirmed).
    • Hunger drops 3 points every 60 seconds: 3 is from the old page, the 60 seconds is the Hercules default.
    • Intimacy per feeding (40 / 30 / 20): from the old page; the emulators have no per-tier values.

    Food and accessory review against Hercules¶

    • Gremlin food Cheap Lubricant (25232), Willow Fine-grained Trunk (1066), Roween Rotten Meat (7564): taken from Hercules.
    • Wanderer, Whisper, Diabolic and Rigid Nightmare Terror use Pet Food in game; Domovoi uses Luxurious Pet Food.
    • Incubus food is Vital Flower (Yellow) (6110) and Succubus is Vital Flower (Blue) (6113).
    • Skelion food 11616 is Delicious Meat in game (Hercules calls it Yummy Meat).
    • Bug to report: Teddy Bear's food shows as Cotton Wads in game, but item 25233 is named Cotton Tufts.

    • Whisper accessory is Pet Soul Ring (10027). Scatleton's Red Bell Necklace (10040) has no description, so it is not listed.

    • NPC-crafted taming items (Monster Tamer in Alberta and Izlude, Munak's Grandma in Comodo): Orc Trophy 635, No Recipient 636, Book of the Devil 642 and Her Heart 659. Singing Flower 629 and Monster Juice 626 are also crafted there but drop from monsters, so the page does not list the recipe. Recipes from the Hercules scripts (monstertamers.txt) and Tame Ingredients.

    • Knife, Flail and Hammer Goblin rings and the Goblin Leader Staff Of Leader are event only.
