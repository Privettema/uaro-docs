    Endless Tower¶

    Last updated: October 16, 2026

    A team-based instance where a party climbs the tower floor by floor, clearing every monster on a floor to open the way up. Naght Sieger waits at the very top.

    Quick Facts

    • Location: Captain Janssen in Prontera (/navi prontera 217/187), then Misty Island
    • Party Size: 6 person minimum
    • Base Level: 85
    • Time Limit: 4 hours
    • Run Limit: 3 per week (Instance Guide)

    Rules¶

    Rule Detail
    Death Normal: players can be resurrected and come back into the instance.
    Re-entry Original members of the party may leave the dungeon and re-enter as long as a member is still active within the dungeon. If all members exit, the instance will be deleted and the weekly run is consumed.
    Storage Not available inside the instance.
    RODEX Available inside the instance.
    Loot Drops as normal.
    Run Unlock Speak to Lost Soul.
    Party Follow Original members who leave and re-enter can rejoin the party's current floor without climbing back up, which makes restocking quick.
    Level Skip Not available.
    Clearing a Floor Every monster on a floor must be defeated before the gate to the next floor opens. Taming a monster doesn't count as defeating it.

    Getting There¶

    To get to the instance starting point, talk to Captain Janssen in Prontera (/navi prontera 217/187). Speak with him multiple times to initiate the journey.

    Once you unlock the Warp Helper on the island, Warpra takes you to Misty Island for 5,000z.

    A Kafra NPC on the island provides:

    • Personal Storage
    • Guild Storage
    • Return to Prontera

    On Misty Island, locate the Tower Protection Stone NPC and select Enter Endless Tower. All party members must be present on Misty Island when the Party Leader clicks the stone to start the dungeon.

    After the instance is initiated, every party member must type !ready in public chat within 60 seconds. If anyone doesn't, the whole party returns to their save point and the instance must be restarted. Read the Instance Guide for run limits and completion.

    Rewards¶

    After Naght Sieger falls, speak to the Lost Soul to exit; this is what unlocks your next weekly run.

    If you carry both Twin Edge of Naght Sieger swords (13412 and 13413), the Lost Soul combines them into a Violet Fear (1185) two-handed sword. Any refine or card on the originals is lost in the process.
