    Patch Notes - July 21, 2026¶

    Important

    Make sure your client is patched using the patcher - this is important to avoid in-game errors and crashes.

    🎮 Gameplay¶

    Change Description
    Eternal Bastion Weekly Runs Eternal Bastion can now be run 2 times per week (up from 1). All mechanics remain the same
    Quick Cooking Cook the same dish 4 times in a row (each within 5 seconds of the last) and Quick Cooking activates, announced in chat. While active, using a cooking kit instantly cooks that dish again with no menu selection, as long as you have the materials. Normal success rates still apply. Waiting more than 5 seconds or cooking a different dish deactivates it
    Misty Island Warp Misty Island added to Warpra's dungeon list, with a return Warpra on the island itself
    1 Billion Zeny Token Bill Doors in Prontera (100, 253) exchanges 1,000,000,000 zeny for a 1B Zeny Token and back, letting you store zeny above the 2 billion carry cap as an item

    ⚔️ Skills¶

    Berserk Rework (Lord Knight)¶

    Berserk has been reworked. Core numbers are unchanged (HP x3, +100% damage, ASPD +30%, 5 minute duration, HP drain). What changed:

    • Fly Wing, Novice Fly Wing, and Infinite Fly Wing can now be used while Berserked. All other items remain blocked.
    • You can now chat while Berserked.
    • If Concentration is active when you cast Berserk, it is refreshed and extended to 2.6x its normal duration (Lv5: from 45 to 117 seconds). Concentration now ends when Berserk ends.
    • New visuals: the red body tint is replaced with an aura effect and a cast sound. You can hide the aura on your own character via the Status Color Effect setting.

    Monk & Champion Combo SP Costs¶

    Monk and Champion combo skill SP costs have been reduced by roughly half:

    Skill Before After
    Raging Quadruple Blow 11/12/13/14/15 SP 2/4/6/8/10 SP
    Raging Thrust 11/12/13/14/15 SP 2/4/6/8/10 SP
    Raging Palm Strike 2/4/6/8/10 SP 1/2/4/6/8 SP
    Glacier Fist 4/6/8/10/12 SP 2/3/4/5/6 SP
    Chain Crush Combo 4-22 SP 2-11 SP (spirit sphere cost unchanged)

    Settings¶

    Change Description
    Status Color Effect "Buff Yellow Effect" renamed to Status Color Effect in @settings for classes with applicable skills. No mechanical change

    🎒 Items¶

    Bio Lab 4 Equipment Revamp¶

    Underused Bio Lab 4 equipment received buffs:

    Item Changes
    Cannon Spear Now also increases Holy Cross damage by 3% per refine
    Red Ether Bag LUK +15 added
    Mental Stick ASPD +10% added; refine bonuses raised to +15% MATK at +7 and a further +20% at +10 (55% total MATK at +10, up from 35%)
    Sura's Rampage At +7 an extra +5% Finger Offensive and Investigate damage, at +10 a further +10% (up to +35% each)
    Catapult Reworked - +10% Double Strafe base, an extra +10% DS and +10% ASPD at +7, and a further +20% DS and +10% ASPD at +10 (max +40% DS / +20% ASPD)
    Black Wing Base ATK raised from 150 to 180; Back Stab bonus per refine above +5 raised from 2% to 5% (up to +50% total at +10)
    Green Whistle / Stem Whip Refine bonuses added - an extra +10% Arrow Vulcan damage at +7 and a further +20% at +10 (up to +60% total)
    Dance Shoes ASPD bonus raised from 3% to 5%; the -5 SP cost discount moved from Throw Arrow to Arrow Vulcan

    Other Item Changes¶

    Change Description
    Telekinetic Orb Slotting Telekinetic Orb can now have a slot added at the Seiyablem slotting NPC (400,000 zeny + 1 Elunium, 20% success; failure destroys the item)
    Green Operation Coat Enchants Green Operation Coat added to the Apprentice Craftsman's High Grade Armor stat enchant service in Prontera
    Bastion Enchants for All Mid Headgears Bastion enchants (Master Craftsman in Prontera) can now be applied to any headgear that occupies the middle slot, including multi-slot headgears. Previously only mid-slot-only headgears qualified

    🏯 WoE & GvG¶

    Change Description
    Butterfly Wing Teleport Delay Using a Butterfly Wing, Novice Butterfly Wing, or Infinite Butterfly Wing on GvG maps (including castles during WoE and KoE) now starts a 3 second countdown before you return to your save point, regardless of combat status. This replaces the post-combat save point delay introduced last week
    Infinite Butterfly Wing Rules Infinite Butterfly Wing now follows the same rules as regular Butterfly Wings: unusable during duels and on no-return maps
    Item Block Review All consumables, items, and card effects have been reviewed for proper blocking within WoE, pre-trans WoE, and PvP arenas
    Morocc NPCs Relocated The WoE Information board and the Repairman in Morocc were relocated (now at (225, 65) and (220, 61))

    🐾 Pets¶

    Change Description
    New Pet: Valkyrie Randgris Valkyrie Randgris now has a chance to drop the Randgris Egg. With its pet accessory equipped, the pet grants +20% physical and magical damage against Dark and Neutral element enemies at Loyal intimacy (+15% at Cordial)
    Smaller Follow Egg Sprites Follow pet egg sprites made smaller: Randgris, Orc Hero, Dark Lord, Dark Illusion, Baphomet, Kiel, Gloom
    Mute Pets Added a Mute Pets option in @settings that hides all pet talk and emotes for you only. Pets still function normally and other players are unaffected

    🛠️ Fixes¶

    Fix Description
    String Buf Errors Fixed multiple client-side "String Buf" errors with equipment property actions
    Quest Info Verbiage Ongoing quest info displayed for Nidhoggur's Nest and Eternal Bastion updated to reflect proper cooldown verbiage
    Muff's Loan Quest Repaired the Muff's Loan quest: a bug caused several NPCs mid-quest to stop responding, dead-ending progress. Dorian's dialogue now correctly asks for 5 Red Gemstones (the amount actually required all along). The final reward is now 1 Old Blue Box + 2 Yggdrasil Seeds (down from 4 seeds)
