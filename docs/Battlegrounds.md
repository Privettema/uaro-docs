### Capture the Flag

**Capture the Flag** is a new mode added to the normal Battleground rotation. Carry the enemy flag
to your own base marker to score:

- First team to `2` captures wins. If the `15` minute timer runs out, the team with the highest
  score takes the win.
- Taking or returning a flag is a `3` second channel and only one player can work a flag at a
  time. Touching your own flag returns it instantly, but only while it is lying dropped.
- While carrying a flag you cannot attack or use skills, your move speed is locked at `175`
  (**Increase AGI** and speed potions have no effect) and a marker floats above your head.
- Dying drops the flag on the spot. A dropped flag returns to base after `60` seconds, or
  immediately if the carrier logs out or leaves the match.
- Respawns are wave based: you wait in your team pen and everyone is healed, buffed and warped
  to the front line every `15` seconds.
- Rewards: `15` **Valor Badges** for a win, `9` for a draw and `7` for a loss, same as
  **Flavius** and **Team Deathmatch**.
