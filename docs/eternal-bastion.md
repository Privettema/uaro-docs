# Eternal Bastion

The ultimate PvE endgame challenge on uaRO: a party of 12 fights through 100 waves of escalating enemies, ending with a randomly selected final boss.

!!! info "Quick Facts"
    - **Location:** Bastion NPC in Veins, the Canyon Village (`/navi veins 218/136`)
    - **Party Size:** 12 players required
    - **Base Level:** 85
    - **Time Limit:** 4 hours, the instance fails if the timer runs out
    --8<-- "instances.md:fact-eternal-bastion"

## Rules

<div class="uaro-nowrap-first-col" markdown>

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Death** | Permadeath: you are warped out with no resurrection inside (see [Permadeath & Extra Lives](#permadeath-extra-lives)). |
| **Re-entry** | Once you leave or die out, you cannot return to the instance. Disconnects are the exception: if the run is still active and a party member is still inside, you are returned to your party when you log back in. |
| **Storage** | Accessible waves 1–79, NPC destroyed at wave 80+. |
| **RODEX** | Not available inside the instance. |
| **Loot** | No loot drops from wave mobs. Rewards come from the milestone boxes (see [Rewards](#rewards)). |
--8<-- "instances.md:rule-unlock-eternal-bastion"
| **Party Lock** | If party composition changes at any point, the instance resets and is destroyed. |

</div>

## Getting There

Talk to the **Bastion** NPC in Veins (`/navi veins 218/136`) to start the run.

## Permadeath & Extra Lives

!!! danger "Permadeath"
    If you die, you are warped out of the instance. There is **no resurrection** inside the instance. Once you're out, you're out. Come prepared or don't come at all.

Milestone rewards also grant extra lives to each party member:

- **Wave 75:** each member gains 1 extra life
- **Wave 90:** each member gains 1 additional extra life (max 2)

When you die with an extra life, you revive at full HP after a 3-second cooldown. When you die with no extra lives remaining, you are permanently removed from the instance.

## Wave Mechanics

<div class="uaro-nowrap-first-col" markdown>

| Mechanic | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Wave Timer** | 120 seconds per wave, 300 seconds for Wave 100. |
| **Skip Vote** | `!skip` command (majority vote >50%) to advance, which stacks current and next wave mobs for faster clearing. Disabled at milestone waves. |
| **Status Effects** | From wave 61, a 50% chance every 60 seconds of Curse, Silence, Confusion, Blind or Bleeding (10 seconds, unavoidable). |
| **Meteor Storm** | From wave 81, a 25% chance every 60 seconds of volcanic strikes around each player: 3-5 bursts with 2-second intervals. |

</div>

## Monsters

Waves escalate as the run goes on, ending with a randomly selected boss at Wave 100.

### Wave Progression

| Waves | Difficulty | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **1–20** | Beginner | Porings, Fabres, Lunatics — a warm-up phase to ease into the challenge |
| **21–40** | Intermediate | Enemies start to hit harder |
| **41–60** | Advanced | Expect tougher enemies with more HP |
| **61–80** | Elite | High HP, strong AoE attacks, and random status effects on the map |
| **81–99** | Endgame | Bio 4 / HTF / OGH monsters — high-damage mobs with complex mechanics |
| **100** | Final Boss | A unique MVP with devastating skills, randomly selected from the boss pool |
