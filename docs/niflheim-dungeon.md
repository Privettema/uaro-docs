# Niflheim Dungeon

Beneath the realm of the dead lies **Niflheim Dungeon**, two floors of restless spirits watched over by the **Death Witch**.

!!! info "Quick Facts"
    - **Location:** Niflheim, `/navi niflheim 149/124`
    - **Level Required:** Base Level `99`
    - **Floors:** `2`
    - **MVP:** **Death Witch**, on floor `2`

## Getting There

The entrance is in **Niflheim** at `/navi niflheim 149/124`. You need Base Level `99` to enter.

**Teleport**, **Fly Wing**, **Warp Portal** and **Ice Wall** don't work inside. **Butterfly Wing** works.
