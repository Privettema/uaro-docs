# Patch Notes - September 29, 2026

!!! warning "Important"
    Make sure your client is patched using the patcher -
    this is important to avoid in-game errors and crashes.

---

## 🎯 NPC

### The Sign Quest

**The Sign Quest** has been reworked so a wrong answer no longer leaves you stuck:

- Failing part **6A** / **6B** by choosing the wrong option now puts you on a `15` minute cooldown.
- Once the cooldown ends, talk to **Gen** in **Niflheim** to retrieve your **Symbol of the Nine Realms**
  and enter again via the applicable method (**6A** / **6B**).
- Failing the quest no longer grants EXP.
- A GM is no longer needed to manually reset your quest when you choose incorrectly.

### Onward to the New World

**Onward to the New World** keeps the same intent as the original quest: only the party leader / quest
initiator receives credit for the quest upon reaching the required kill count.

- The map is now instanced, so multiple parties and players can start and complete the quest at the same
  time instead of one at a time.
- The party leader starts the test, and all party members must be on the same map.
- Required kills changed from `40` to `50` **Allied Soldiers**. Progress is now shown in the quest log.
- The time limit changed from `15` to `20` minutes.
- Monsters now respawn instantly throughout the map upon being killed.

## 🐾 Pets

### Pet Switch

Using a **Pet Incubator** while a pet is out now lets you swap directly to another pet egg:

- Your current pet returns to its egg (requires `1` free inventory slot).
- The **Pet Incubator** is now only consumed when you select an egg. Closing the list no longer wastes it.
- Your **Pet Stylist** skin is kept when switching pets.
