## ⚔️ Battlegrounds

### Capture the Flag

**Capture the Flag** is a new mode added to the normal Battleground rotation. Carry the enemy flag
to your own base marker to score:

- First team to `2` captures wins. If the `15` minute timer runs out, the team with the highest
  score takes the win.
- Taking or returning a flag is a `3` second channel and only one player can work a flag at a
  time. Touching your own flag returns it instantly, but only while it is lying dropped.
- While carrying a flag you cannot attack or use skills, your move speed is locked at `175`
  (**Increase AGI** and speed potions have no effect) and a marker floats above your head.
- Dying drops the flag on the spot. A dropped flag returns to base after `60` seconds, or
  immediately if the carrier logs out or leaves the match.
- Respawns are wave based: you wait in your team pen and everyone is healed, buffed and warped
  to the front line every `15` seconds.
- Rewards: `15` **Valor Badges** for a win, `9` for a draw and `7` for a loss, same as
  **Flavius** and **Team Deathmatch**.

### Meltdown (Whitesmith)

**Meltdown** has been reworked into a PvE debuff skill:

- Cast time removed. SP cost remains at `50`.
- Duration increased: now `25` seconds at level `1`, scaling up to `150` seconds at level `10`
  to match **Adrenaline Rush**.
- On hit, imposes a `5` second debuff at a `100%` rate on PvE targets, reducing the target's
  DEF and decreasing monster ATK by `35%`. Works with **Cart Termination**, and hitting the
  target again within the `5` seconds refreshes the duration.
- The debuff still does **not** work on MVPs, but it does affect mini-boss type monsters.
- The `5` second debuff length is the same at every skill level, and it procs on normal
  attacks as well as skills.
- Equipment breaking in PvP is unchanged.

## 🎒 Items

### Avarice (Whitesmith Platinum Skill)

Whitesmiths can now trade their **Bag of Gold Coins** for the new platinum skill **Avarice**:

- Requires an additional `200,000,000z` upfront cost.
- Upon confirmation, the **Bag of Gold Coins** is deleted permanently and the platinum skill
  **Avarice** is granted.
- Removes the zeny cost of **Mammonite** and **Cart Termination** permanently, for that
  character only.
- With the bag gone, there is no longer a weight detriment tied to the cost reduction.
- Survives skill resets: **Galdur** and the **Platinum Skill** NPC re-grant it automatically.

!!! note "The Lesser Version Still Works"
    If you don't want to spend the extra `200,000,000z`, keeping the **Bag of Gold Coins**
    (`50,000,000z` from **Galdur**) in your inventory still drops **Mammonite** and
    **Cart Termination** to `500z` per cast, at the price of `500` weight. The bag is now
    limited to one per character.
