## 🎮 Gameplay

### Bombring Event

**Bombring** replaces the old **Run or Die** event. Last player standing wins:

- The arena floor detonates in telegraphed blast patterns that grow bigger and faster every round
- Everyone is locked to the same movement speed and skills are disabled — dodging is all that counts
- Runs every odd hour: announced at `:27`, starts at `:30`. Minimum `4` players
- The last survivor wins `75` <img src="../../../img/7539.gif" alt="" style="vertical-align:middle;width:1.25rem;height:1.25rem;"> **Poring Coins**

### Events

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **`@event` Command** | `@bombring` and `@dice` replaced with a single **`@event`** command — join open registrations, see which event is running, and check the schedule of all `4` automated events |
| **Event Respawn** | Dying in **Bombring** and respawning returns you to the spot you joined from with the HP you had, instead of your save point at `1` HP |

### Guild Management

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **`@guild` Command** | All guild functions merged into **`@guild`**: Storage Logs, Bank Logs (moved out of the **Guild Agent** NPC), Request Tokens, and a leader-only Management menu with every permission setting per position. Current guild bank balance is shown on open for members with bank access. `@guildbank` and `@guildlog` removed |
| **Guild Disband** | Disbanding a guild now clears its guild bank — withdraw everything first |

### Dressing Room

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Free Stylist** | A free **Stylist** inside the Dressing Room lets you preview hair styles, hair colors, and cloth colors, and mix them with costume previews. Your real look is restored when you leave or log out |
