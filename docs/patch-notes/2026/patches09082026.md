# Patch Notes - September 8, 2026

!!! warning "Important"
    Make sure your client is patched using the patcher -
    this is important to avoid in-game errors and crashes.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Party Loot Notifications** | **Tiger's Footskin**, **Young Twig**, **Level 10 Cookbook** and **Heroic Emblem** have been added to the party loot notification list |
| **Inn Minimap Icons** | Inn icons have been added to the minimap of towns that did not have them yet, for easier visibility |

---

## ⚔️ Skills

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Magic Crasher** | Now pierces `75%` of the DEF of non-player monsters and its damage has been doubled. Damage still calculates cards and uses the active property on the weapon (scrolls/converters) |

---

## 🎒 Items

| Item | Changes |
|------|---------|
| **Catapult** | Reworked (**Bio Lab 4** shop) - DEX `+2`. At `+7` or higher, `+10%` Ranged Physical damage and `+10%` ASPD. At `+8` or higher, enables use of **Long Range Snatcher** when auto attacking. At `+10`, a further `+10%` Ranged Physical damage and `+10%` ASPD (max `+20%` Ranged Physical / `+20%` ASPD). All other stats remain the same |

---

## 🏰 Instances

### Eternal Bastion

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Wave Spawns** | Waves `86`, `88` and `89` now spawn their monsters correctly |
| **Disconnect Recovery** | Disconnecting during a run now returns you to your party inside the instance when you log back in, as long as the run is still active and a party member is still inside |

---

## 👾 Monsters

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Thief Bug Spawns** | **Thief Bug** spawns have been removed from **Payon Inn** and **Jawaii** |

---

## 🎯 NPC

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Midgard Expedition Camp** | **Midgard Allied Forces Post** is now called **Midgard Expedition Camp** in the **Warper** menu |
| **Morroc Field** | **Morocc Field** has been corrected to **Morroc Field** in the **Warper** menu |

---

## 🛠️ Fixes

| Fix | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Headgear Layer Priority** | Fixed the layer priority for the `C_RA_Hair_PK` headgear |
| **Autobonus Timers** | Autobonus script timers now refresh to reflect the gear currently equipped, negating any prior delayed queues |
| **Hunting Missions Shop** | The **Hunting Missions** shop now checks your weight and free inventory space before taking **Mission Points**. Previously it could take the points and drop the items on the ground |
