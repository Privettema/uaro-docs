## 🏰 Dungeons

### Niflheim Dungeon

**Niflheim Dungeon** is now open. The entrance is in **Niflheim**: `/navi niflheim 149/124`.

- Requires Base Level `99`.
- **Teleport**, **Fly Wing**, **Warp Portal** and **Ice Wall** are disabled. **Butterfly Wing** works.

| Floor | Monsters |
|-------|----------|
| **Floor 1** | Gan Ceann, Brutal Murderer, Ghost Cube, Lude Gal |
| **Floor 2** | Disguiser, Grote, Pierrotzoist, Blue Moon Loli Ruri |
| **MVP** | Death Witch on floor `2` |

!!! note "Keep Your Loot"
    - **Shadowdecon**, **Zelunium** and their ores drop from every monster.
    - **Witch Necklace** drops from **Death Witch**.

    We will be looking at the stats and making this loot useful over the next few updates, so don't throw it away.

## ⚔️ Battlegrounds

Initial release of the **Battlegrounds** changes and improvements. The system has been reworked and moved into the server core, greatly improving performance, and updated to match the 2025 client. Various other improvements are in the works, including a BG Ranking system.

### Queue System

- The proper BG queue system is now working.
- Queueing is now allowed during a running match: select the option to join the current or the next match.
- Overflow players now stay queued.
- If you're dead, trading, vending, etc. when the match triggers, you will not be pulled in and will stay queued.
- Re-queue cooldown after leaving the queue or logging out while queued reduced from `60` to `5` seconds.

### Win Streak Bonuses

Winning matches in a row now pays bonus **Valor Badges**:

| Win Streak | Bonus |
|------------|-------|
| **`3`-`4` wins in a row** | `2` bonus badges per win |
| **`5` or more wins in a row** | `3` bonus badges per win |

- Late joiners get half the streak bonus (rounded down).
- The streak ends if you lose, draw or leave the match before it ends (including an AFK kick).

### Respawn Timers

Respawn timers (from death to the transfer back into the match) have been reduced:

| Respawn | Before | After |
|---------|--------|-------|
| **First Respawn** | `30` seconds | `20` seconds |
| **Later Respawns** | `15` seconds | `12` seconds |

Applies to **Tierra Gorge**, **Flavius**, **Emperium Rush** and **Time & Space Anomaly**. **Capture the Flag** respawn waves are also reduced from `15` to `12` seconds.

### Other Changes

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Homunculus Kills** | Kills by homunculi now count towards the owning player |
| **Team Colors** | Swapped to match lore: **Guillaume** = blue, **Croix** = red. **Flavius** crystals swapped to match |
| **Battle Therapist** | The heal NPC now grants Super Novices a temporary `10` second **Super Novice Spirit** link on use |
| **Tierra Gorge** | Late joiners now spawn at the entry point instead of the waiting room |
| **Time & Space Anomaly** | The scoreboard now shows damage in units of `100k` |
| **AFK Check** | Active songs no longer count as being AFK. This does not protect you if you are reported for abusing the AFK mechanic |
| **Guild Chat** | Guild chat is no longer blocked, and typing into guild chat no longer auto-routes to BG team chat |

### BG Fixes

| Fix | Description |

| **Team Leader** | Fixed the team leader not being properly transferred when the leader leaves. A random player remaining on the team becomes the new leader |

## 🎮 Gameplay

### Ping

Added the **Ping** skill to all classes. It is located in the 1st class skill tab and is obtained at Base Level `50` or higher. The skill has `2` selectable levels:

| Level | Ping | Who Sees It |
|-------|------|-------------|
| `1` | **Party Ping** | All party members. Not visible to non-members |
| `2` | **Guild Leader Ping** | All guildmates, regardless of party. Not visible to non-guild members. Only the guild leader can use it |

- In **Battlegrounds**, only the team leader can ping, and the ping is seen by the team.
- Pings are only seen by members on the same map.

### Hide Cart

Added an option for merchant classes to hide their cart: **Hide Cart** in `@settings`, under character settings.

- While your cart is hidden, you can't open it with `Alt+W`. Unhide it first.
- Skills that require a cart, such as **Vending** and **Cart Boost**, work as normal, as if you still have a cart.
- The cart is hidden only for you. Other players still see it.
