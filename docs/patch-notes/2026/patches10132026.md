# Patch Notes - October 13, 2026

!!! warning "Important"
    Make sure your client is patched using the patcher -
    this is important to avoid in-game errors and crashes.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

### Scaraba Hole 2 Alternative Access

**Scaraba Hole** `2` now has an alternative way in, so alts on the same account can get access:

- Talk to the **Vigilantes** inside **Scaraba Hole** `1`.
- Requires the standard **Light of El Dicastes** (`2844`) equipped.
- No quest variable or **Ring of the Ancient Wise King** is needed.
- Slotted and enchanted versions of the accessory do not work.
- The personal storage restriction on **Light of El Dicastes** has been lifted. Guild storage and trade remain blocked.
- The rest of the quest is unchanged. This is an addition to the standard process for alt access on the same account.

!!! warning "Already Upgraded Your Accessory?"
    If you already progressed past your initial accessory, you will need to complete the chain on an additional
    character. There will be no restores back to its original form.

### Vending & Buying Store Limits

**Vending**, **Autotrade** and **Buying Stores** now allow only `1` entry per stackable item ID per account / hardware ID.

Attempting to bypass this feature, like any other game mechanic, may result in punishment if evidence supports it.

### Visual AFK Status

Characters who are idle now show an **AFK** sprite above their head:

- Uses the same AFK rules as the autoloot settings: the status appears after `60` seconds idle.
- While AFK, the sprite temporarily replaces your upper headgear look.
- Any action that counts as input for the autoloot settings removes the AFK status immediately.
- **Exceptions:** towns (including town interiors), **Autotrade** and open vending / buying stores, and **WoE** castles.

## 🎯 NPC

### Slot Machine

The **Slot Machine** has been updated, and **Glass of Illusion** has been swapped out for **Giant Fly Wing Box[50]**:

- The dialog now stays open after each spin, so you can keep spinning.
- **Giant Fly Wing Box[50]** chance: `2%`.
- **Big Defense Potion** / **Big Magic Defense Potion** chance raised from `4%` to `4.5%`.
- Requires `3` free inventory slots and enough free weight before spinning.
- The Jackpot (`1,000` **Poring Coins**) is now announced server-wide.
