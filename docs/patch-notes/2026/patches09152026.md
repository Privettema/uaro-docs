# Patch Notes - September 15, 2026

!!! warning "Important"
    Make sure your client is patched using the patcher -
    this is important to avoid in-game errors and crashes.

---

## 🗺️ El Dicastes

**El Dicastes** is now open. The new continent, its monsters, quests and rewards are covered on
their own page: [El Dicastes](../../El_Dicastes.md)

### New World Travel

New World travel (**Manuk**, **Splendide** and **El Dicastes**) now runs through **Cat Hand Services**
instead of the **Warper**:

- The Mid Camp Kafra has been replaced by **Fluffy Gyaruk** at the **Midgard Expedition Camp**.
  A one-time contract of `200` **Delicious Fish**, `20` **Fish Tail** and `550,000` zeny unlocks
  a save point, storage, guild storage and teleports.
- **Manuk** and **Splendide** have been removed from the **Warper** dungeon list.
- **Inn Attendants** have been added to **El Dicastes**, **Manuk** and **Splendide**.

---

## ⚔️ Skills

### Auto Spell (Sage)

**Auto Spell** (Hindsight) now offers **Earth Spike** instead of **Frost Diver**:

- Available from **Auto Spell** level `2` (casts **Earth Spike** Lv`1`), level `3` (Lv`2`) and Lv`5`
  from **Auto Spell** level `4` onwards.
- Under **Sage Spirit** it is always cast at Lv`5`.
- **Frost Diver** has been removed from the **Auto Spell** list entirely.

## 🎮 Gameplay

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Map EXP Bonus** | **Bio Lab 4** has been added to the `@mapexp` bonus, so it now covers the entire dungeon |
| **Bio Lab 3 Boss** | The **Bio Lab 3** boss now spawns at one of `6` spots on the map instead of always the same place |

### MVP Tomb Rework

The MVP tombs of **Lord of the Dead**, **Bio Lab 3**, **Bio Lab 4**, **Thanatos** and
**Wounded Satan Morroc** have been reworked:

- Each of them now has a permanent tomb sitting at a fixed spot on its map, so you can read the last
  killer, the top damage dealers and the time of death at any point without hunting for the tomb.
- Their respawn timers now survive a server restart instead of resetting.
- These five no longer appear on **Convex Mirror**.
- Tombs now appear `3` seconds after the kill instead of `10`.
- Their MVP kill announcement no longer fires twice.

## 🎮 Commands

### Vending Import

The vending and buying store **Import** function has been restored to the client. It also works
with **`@at`**:

- Imports all variables, including the shop name, items and prices, from your most recent vend.
- Tied to the character itself, not the account, if you have multiple vendors on the same account.
- Items that are no longer available (moved, sold, bought etc.) are removed from the imported list.

### Vending Search

- **`@whosell`** and **`@whobuy`** name searches now match every listed item containing the name,
  not just one.
- They now reply "No one is currently selling/buying that item" instead of "Invalid item" when the
  item exists but nobody has it listed.
