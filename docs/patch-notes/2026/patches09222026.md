### Return to El Dicastes

The **Light of El Dicastes** accessory now grants the skill **Return to El Dicastes**:

- Cooldown of `16` minutes `40` seconds.
- Blocked in **Battlegrounds** and **GvG**, and on maps where warping or teleporting is disabled.

### Monster Drops

The `10` item default cap on monster kill drop items and cards has been lifted. A duplicate of the same
card or equipment is still null and void, and only the highest `%` is taken.

## 🏯 WoE

- Mercenaries can no longer be used in **WoE**. Entering a castle dismisses your mercenary and returns the
  scroll to your inventory.
- **Pre-Trans WoE** now also removes stat food buffs when you enter a castle.
- Castle restrictions are now applied when **WoE** starts and cleared when it ends. **WoE SE** castles are
  no longer restricted outside of WoE hours.
