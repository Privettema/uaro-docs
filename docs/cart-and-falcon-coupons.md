# Cart & Falcon Coupons

Cart Unlock Coupons and Falcon Coupons unlock new cart and falcon appearances. Buy them with
**Cash Points** in the [Cash Shop](donations.md#cash-shop).

- **1 coupon = 1 appearance**
- Unlocks are **permanent**, but only for the **character** you unlock them on, not your account
- You can switch between unlocked appearances at any time

---

## 🦅 Falcon Coupon

Redeem Falcon Coupons at the **Retired Ranger** outside the Hunter Guild in Hugel
(`/navi hugel 209, 213`).

### Requirements

- A **Sniper** with the **Falcon Mastery** skill
- A Falcon rented from the **Falcon Breeder** in the Hunter Guild (`/navi hugel_in 381, 304`)
- A **Falcon Coupon**

| Retired Ranger | Falcon Breeder |
| :---: | :---: |
| ![Retired Ranger NPC](img/Coupons/Retired-Ranger-NPC.png) | ![Falcon Breeder NPC](img/Coupons/Falcon-Breeder-NPC.png) |

### How to Redeem

1. Talk to the **Retired Ranger**.
2. Choose **swap Falcons**, or redeem a coupon to unlock a new appearance.

### Available Falcons

Snipers start with the **Eagle**. Coupons unlock three more: the Hunter default **Falcon**,
**Hawk**, and **Owl**. Swapping between unlocked appearances is free.

| Falcon | Eagle | Hawk | Owl |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| ![Falcon](img/Coupons/Falcon-Default.png) | ![Eagle](img/Coupons/Falcon-Eagle.png) | ![Hawk](img/Coupons/Falcon-Hawk.png) | ![Owl](img/Coupons/Falcon-Owl.png) |

!!! important
    Other players see your Falcon appearance, even if you hide your own Falcon in `@settings`.

## 🎟️ Cart Unlock Coupon

### Requirements

- A cart rented from a **Kafra** (**Merchant** classes and **Super Novice**)
- The **Change Cart 2** platinum skill, from the **Platinum Skill** NPC in the
  [Main Office](main-office.md)
- A **Cart Unlock Coupon** in your inventory

### How to Redeem

Use **Change Cart 2** at level 2 with the coupon in your inventory, then pick a cart from the prompt.
