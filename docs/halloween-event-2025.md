## Quests

=== "Main Quest"

    Talk to **Grave-Herald** at `/navi niflheim 288/269` to start.

    !!! info "Once per account"
        Only **one character per account** can complete the main quest. Finishing it unlocks the daily quests for
        **all** characters on your account, so choose your strongest character.

    **Part 1: Field hunt** (Level 50+)

    1. Accept the quest from **Grave-Herald**.
    2. Go to `nif_fild01` and talk to **Apprentice Grave-Keeper** at `/navi nif_fild01 234/249`.
    3. Hunt in `nif_fild01` and `nif_fild02`: 60 Garling and 20 Skelion (10 on each field).
    4. Return to the Apprentice Grave-Keeper.

    **Rewards:** 35,000 Base EXP, 17,500 Job EXP, 20 Halloween Coins

    **Part 2: Dungeon hunt**

    1. Enter Niflheim Dungeon F1 (`nif_dun01`).
    2. Hunt 30 Ghost Cube, 20 Gan Ceann and 15 Brutal Murderer.
    3. Return to **Grave-Herald** in Niflheim.

    **Rewards:** 70,000 Base EXP, 35,000 Job EXP, 70 Halloween Coins, 35 Deviling Coins

    ![Main Quest NPCs](img/Halloween/halloween-main.webp)

=== "Daily Quests"

    Talk to **Event Wizard** at `/navi nif_fild01 233/227` after completing the main quest on any character.

    - Up to **4 quests per day**, resetting at `00:00` server time (check with `@time`)
    - Only **one active quest at a time** per account
    - Pick **Take Daily Quest**, choose Normal or Hard, hunt **30** of the assigned monster, then pick
      **Claim Rewards**
    - You can abandon a quest and take another, but its progress is lost

    | | Normal Mode | Hard Mode |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Level** | 70-90 | 95+ |
    | **Where** | Fields (`nif_fild01`/`nif_fild02`) or Dungeon F1 (`nif_dun01`) | Dungeon F2 (`nif_dun02`) only |
    | **Entry cost** | Free | `25,000` Zeny |
    | **Monsters** | Skelion, Garling, Ghost Cube, Lude Gal, Gan Ceann, Brutal Murderer | Disguiser, Blue Moon, Grote, Pierrotzoist |
    | **Halloween Coins** | ![Halloween Coins](img/Halloween/halloweenCoin.gif){ width="20" } 35-49 | ![Halloween Coins](img/Halloween/halloweenCoin.gif){ width="20" } 50-70 |
    | **Deviling Coins** | ![Deviling Coins](img/Halloween/dev-coin.png){ width="20" } 15-22 | ![Deviling Coins](img/Halloween/dev-coin.png){ width="20" } 25-35 |
    | **Black Soul** | ![Black Soul](img/Halloween/blackSoul.gif){ width="20" } 12-19 | ![Black Soul](img/Halloween/blackSoul.gif){ width="20" } 18-28 |

    !!! danger "Very strong monsters!"
        Hard Mode is extremely dangerous. A party is recommended.

    !!! tip "Rewards add up"
        Completing all 4 dailies gives about 140-196 Halloween Coins a day on Normal and 200-280 on Hard.

    ![Event Wizard Location](img/Halloween/halloween-quest.webp)

## Tips

| Level | Suggested plan |
|-------|----------------|
| 50-70 | Start the main quest to unlock dailies, farm the fields for Skelion and Garling, and save coins for budget costumes (65-95). |
| 70-90 | Farm Dungeon F1 for better spawn density, do all 4 Normal dailies, and start collecting materials for medium-tier costumes and the Scatelon quest. |
| 95+ | Party up for Hard dailies in F2, farm Black Soul there, and aim for premium costumes. |

- **Halloween Coins:** do all 4 dailies every day.
- **Skelion materials:** farm the fields (`nif_fild01`/`nif_fild02`).
- **Black Soul:** Ghost Cube in F1, or Disguiser and Pierrotzoist in F2.
- **Pumpkin Decor and Cookie Bat:** Garling in the fields or F1.

## FAQ

**Can I do the main quest on multiple characters?**<br>
No, only one character per account can complete it.

**Can all my characters do dailies?**<br>
Yes, once the main quest is done on one character. Only one character can have an active daily at a time.

**How many Scatelon pets can I get?**<br>
One per account. It is the rarest pet of the event.

**Should I save coins or buy costumes now?**<br>
If you want the Scatelon pet, keep 300 Halloween Coins for its quest. Costumes are permanent rewards.

**Is F2 soloable?**<br>
The monsters are level 108-115 and very strong. Some classes can manage, but a party is strongly recommended.

**Can I abandon and retake a daily?**<br>
Yes, but you lose its progress, and you can only complete 4 per day.
