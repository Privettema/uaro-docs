# Battlegrounds

Battlegrounds (BG) are team-based PvP matches where two teams compete to complete objectives and earn **Valor Badges**.

Join Battlegrounds from the **Battlegrounds NPC** in Prontera: `/navi prontera 163, 171`.

* Check the NPC to see the **currently active BG**, join a match, or browse the **BG Shop**.
* You can also use `@bg` to join the BG queue from anywhere.
* Each mode has a minimum number of players that must queue before the round will start.
* Once enough players have joined, you will automatically be placed into the match and warped to the BG map.
* After the match, you will be returned to your **previous location**, not your save point.

!!! warning
    **Use caution when joining with `@bg`** — you will be automatically teleported once a match starts.

---

## BG Happy Hour

Battlegrounds have **3 daily Happy Hours** (Server Time - GMT):

1. **04:00**
2. **12:00**
3. **20:00**

During Happy Hour, **Valor Badge rewards are doubled**. Use Happy Hour to get the most out of your Battleground matches!

!!! note
    Reminder you can check the current server time with `@time`.

## Battleground Modes

### Tierra Gorge

![Tierra Gorge](img/Battlegrounds/tierra-gorge.png){ width="240" }

Destroy the opposing team's **Food Storage** (also called the Storehouse).

* Break through enemy **Barricades** to reach their base.
* Barricades can be rebuilt during the match.
* Capture the **Neutrality Flag** to gain a forward position.
* The team that destroys the enemy Food Storage wins.

### Flavius

![Flavius](img/Battlegrounds/flavius.png){ width="240" }

Destroy the opposing team's **Crystal**.

* Defeat the Crystal's guardians before attacking it.
* Destroying a Crystal scores a point.
* The first team to reach **2 points** wins.

### Team Deathmatch

![Team Deathmatch](img/Battlegrounds/team-deathmatch.png){ width="240" }

Reach the kill score before the other team.

* Defeating a player scores a kill for your team.
* Deaths are important, so stay with your team.
* If the timer expires, the team with the higher score wins. A draw only happens when the scores are exactly tied.

### Emperium Rush

![Emperium Rush](img/Battlegrounds/emperium-rush.png){ width="240" }

Hold the Emperium the longest.

* Emperiums in BG take only **1** damage from any source.
* You cannot damage an Emperium your team already holds to recapture it.

### Krieger von Midgard (KvM)

![Krieger von Midgard (KvM)](img/Battlegrounds/kvm.png){ width="240" }

Annihilate all opponents.

* Each team's score is the number of players still alive, and every death lowers it.
* A team wins at once when the opposing team has no players left.
* If the match timer expires, the team with more survivors wins. Equal counts are a draw.
* Foods stay active when the match starts.
* When you enter, a **Battle Therapist** spawns at both team bases offering **Heal**, **Blessing**, **Increase AGI** and **Storage**. It despawns once the match starts.

### Capture the Flag

![Capture the Flag](img/Battlegrounds/capture-the-flag.png){ width="240" }

Carry the enemy flag back to your team's base to score.

* First team to **2 captures** wins.
* If the **15 minute** timer expires, the team with the highest score wins.
* Picking up or returning a flag requires a **3 second channel**.
* Only one player can interact with a flag at a time.
* Touching your own dropped flag returns it instantly.
* Dying drops the flag where you died.
* A dropped flag returns to its base after **60 seconds**.
* A flag immediately returns to base if its carrier logs out or leaves the match.
* Respawns happen in **15 second waves**.
* Players waiting to respawn are healed and buffed before being warped back to the front line.

While carrying a flag:

* You cannot attack or use skills.
* Move speed is locked at `175`.
* **Increase AGI** and speed potions have no effect.
* A marker is displayed above the carrier.

### Time & Space Anomaly

![Time & Space Anomaly](img/Battlegrounds/time-and-space-anomaly.png){ width="240" }

Deal more damage to the **Anomaly** than the opposing team.

* The match lasts about **8 minutes**. Each team spawns in a separate room, with a **20 second** preparation timer.
* The Anomaly is an MVP that changes **race and element every 2 minutes**, up to Stage 3.
* Your damage increases by **20%** at Stage 2 and **40%** at Stage 3.
* The team that deals the most total damage to the Anomaly wins.
* PvP is enabled between the teams.

## Rewards

Battlegrounds reward **Valor Badges** (item ID `7829`) based on the match result.

| Mode | Win | Draw | Loss |
| - | -: | -: | -: |
| Tierra Gorge | 15 | 8 | 6 |
| Flavius | 12 | 7 | 5 |
| Krieger von Midgard (KvM) | 11 | 7 | 5 |
| Team Deathmatch | 15 | 8 | 6 |
| Emperium Rush | 12 | 7 | 5 |
| Time & Space Anomaly | 15 | 9 | 7 |
| Capture the Flag | 15 | 9 | 7 |

The reward depends on when you joined the match:

* **First third** of the match: full reward.
* **Second third**: about 66% of the reward.
* **Final third**: about 33% of the reward.

Happy Hour and Maroll's Blessing increase these rewards.

## Maroll's Blessing

**Maroll's Blessing** provides a reward bonus for players who have not participated in Battlegrounds for a while.

If you have been away from BG for some time, you may receive **additional rewards** when your match ends.

## BG Shop

The **BG Shop** is available through the Battlegrounds NPC in Prontera. Most gear costs Valor Badges, and supplies cost zeny or Valor Badges.

* Everything in the shop can be traded.
* BG gear can be used in BG, PvE and PvP. It cannot be used in WoE or KoE.
* **Glorious** gear can only be used in BG.
* BG consumables and supplies can be used in BG and PvP. They cannot be used in PvE, WoE or KoE.
* **Always check the item description** for its usage restrictions and limitations.

## Battleground Changes

| Change | Description |
| - | - |
| Entry | Pre-trans characters cannot join. Extended and Trans classes only. |
| Queue cooldown | Joining and then leaving the queue starts a 60 second cooldown. |
| Team balancing | If duplicate classes join, they are split between the teams. |
| Class change cards | Abracadabra and class change cards (Azoth Card, Hylozoist Card) are blocked on battleground maps. |
| Party Buffs | Party buffs cast on a battleground map reach every teammate in range, whether or not you are grouped with them. |
| **Skills** |
| ![cr_devotion](img/Class_Changes/cr_devotion.gif) Devotion | Works in BG without the need for a party. |
| ![tk_highjump](img/WoE/tk_highjump.gif) High Jump | Works in BG. |
| ![tk_run](img/Class_Changes/tk_run.png) Running | Works in BG. |
| ![am_potionpitcher](img/Class_Changes/am_potionpitcher.png) Potion Pitcher | Does not work on battleground barricades, objectives, and flags. |
| **Status Effects** |
| ![dc_dontforgetme](img/Class_Changes/dc_dontforgetme.png){ width="24" } Please Don't Forget Me | Cleared on death and when a battleground ends. |
| ![i_divestarmor](img/Class_Changes/i_divestarmor.png){ width="24" } Full Strip | Cleared on death and when a battleground ends. |
| ![mg_stonecurse](img/Class_Changes/mg_stonecurse.png){ width="24" } Stone Curse | Dispelled automatically when you leave a battleground. |
| ![i_decreaseagi](img/Class_Changes/i_decreaseagi.png){ width="24" } Decrease AGI | Dispelled automatically when you leave a battleground. |
| Entering and leaving | You are fully dispelled when you enter and when you leave. |
