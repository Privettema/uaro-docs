    🍂 October 6, 2026

    • Vending is capped at the NPC price for NPC-sold items, and buying stores can't offer below the NPC sell price.
    • Bard songs and Dancer dances can be removed by Dispell once the target leaves the song area.
    • 18 new costumes in the Cash Shop New tab.
    • +9 or higher Glorious Fist now properly self-casts Zen with Critical Explosion.
    • Fixes: dialogue freezes in Nidhoggur's Nest and with @noks, and Fly Wing dead cells on 20 maps.
    • & more, read the full patch notes

    🍂 September 29, 2026

    • The Sign Quest: failing part 6A/6B now sets a 15 minute cooldown; get the Symbol of the Nine Realms back from Gen in Niflheim, no GM reset needed.
    • Onward to the New World is now instanced: 50 Allied Soldiers in 20 minutes, with progress in the quest log.
    • A Battle Therapist (Heal, Blessing, Increase AGI, Storage) waits at both KvM bases until the match starts.
    • Pet Switch: use a Pet Incubator with a pet out to swap straight to another egg; Pet Stylist skins are kept.
    • Fixes: Survivor's Manteau/Rod combo, Eternal Bastion wave 100 guardian, Hunting Missions EXP, BG Crystal Fragment and Kaite.
    • & more, read the full patch notes

    🍂 September 22, 2026

    • Scaraba Hole is now a Cat Hand destination for 19,000 zeny and joins the @mapexp rotation.
    • Splendide, Manuk and the Midgard Expedition Camp now count as towns, and the camp gains an inn.
    • Light of El Dicastes grants Return to El Dicastes on a 10 minute cooldown.
    • The 10 item cap on monster kill drops is lifted.
    • Scarabas and Dolomedes added to Hunting Missions and the Expanded Token Fragment drop table.
    • & more, read the full patch notes

    🍂 September 15, 2026

    • El Dicastes is open, and New World travel now runs through Cat Hand Services at the Midgard Expedition Camp.
    • Sage Auto Spell offers Earth Spike instead of Frost Diver.
    • Vending and buying store Import restored, and @whosell and @whobuy now match every listed item.
    • MVP tombs reworked for five MVPs: permanent tombs and timers that survive restarts.
    • 18 new Cash Shop costumes.
    • & more, read the full patch notes

    🍂 September 8, 2026

    • Catapult reworked: DEX +2, ranged damage and ASPD bonuses at +7 and +10, and Long Range Snatcher at +8.
    • Magic Crasher pierces 75% of non-player monster DEF and its damage is doubled.
    • Eternal Bastion waves 86, 88 and 89 now spawn, and disconnecting mid-run returns you to your party.
    • Inn icons added to town minimaps, and more items added to party loot notifications.
    • & more, read the full patch notes

    Popular systems¶

    • 💖 Poring Coin System

      Monsters have a 5% chance to drop Poring Coins, which can also be earned through quests and events.

    • 🌀 Warper System

      Dungeon Warper provides easy access to quest locations for all characters on your account.

    • 🐱 Cute Pet System

      Collect evolved pets with unique bonuses to assist you in the game.

    • 🎯 Hunting Missions

      Complete missions for Experience, Zeny, and Mission Points. Available at every Inn.

    • 🎉 Repeatable Quests

      Hunt monsters or collect specific items to earn EXP rewards. These items are tradable and valuable.

    • 🏡 Main Office

      Located in Prontera, exchange Poring Coins, reset stats, and shop for valuable items.

    • 📢 Guild Starter Support System

      Extra support for newly created guilds, so they can progress through PvE at a smooth pace.

    • 💰 Vendor System

      Find, buy and trade items with other players, with safeguards to keep the economy stable.
